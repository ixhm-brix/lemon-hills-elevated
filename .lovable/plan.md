

# Lemon Hills Hotel — Premium Resort Website

## Design Vision
A luxurious, minimal, and elegant single-page website inspired by world-class resorts like Four Seasons and Ritz-Carlton. The design uses a sophisticated palette of **white, soft cream, deep forest green, and subtle gold accents**, with elegant serif headings and clean sans-serif body text. Smooth scroll animations and generous white space create a premium, immersive feel.

---

## Sections

### 1. Hero Section
- Full-width cinematic hero with a stunning hilltop/nature background image
- Tagline: *"Where Luxury Meets Nature"*
- Short elegant description of the resort
- Two CTA buttons: **Book Now** and **Explore Rooms**
- Subtle fade-in animations on load

### 2. About Section
- Brief story of Lemon Hills Hotel emphasizing luxury, comfort, and serenity
- Elegant layout with an accompanying image alongside the text
- Gold accent divider elements

### 3. Rooms & Suites
- 3 room categories displayed as premium cards: **Deluxe Room**, **Executive Suite**, **Presidential Suite**
- Each card features a high-quality image, brief description, price per night, and a "View Details" button
- Clean grid layout with hover effects

### 4. Amenities
- Icon-based grid showcasing: Infinity Pool, Fine Dining, Spa & Wellness, Conference Hall, Free WiFi
- Minimal icons with elegant labels and short descriptions
- Forest green and gold accent styling

### 5. Gallery
- Elegant masonry/grid photo gallery showcasing the resort's beauty
- Uses curated placeholder images of luxury hotel interiors, pools, dining, and landscapes

### 6. Testimonials
- 3 guest reviews with 5-star ratings, guest names, and quotes
- Elegant card design with subtle cream backgrounds

### 7. Booking Section
- Check-in / Check-out date pickers
- Guest count selector
- Prominent **"Reserve Now"** button
- Note: This will be a UI-only form (no backend processing) — can be connected later

### 8. Footer
- Contact info (phone, email, address)
- Embedded location map placeholder
- Social media icon links
- Resort logo and copyright

---

## Design Details
- **Color palette**: White (`#FFFFFF`), Cream (`#FAF7F2`), Deep Forest Green (`#1B3A2D`), Gold (`#C5A572`)
- **Typography**: Serif font (Playfair Display) for headings, sans-serif (Inter) for body
- **Animations**: Smooth scroll navigation, fade-in on scroll for sections
- **Fully responsive** across desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **No backend required** — purely frontend with beautiful static content
